Entry for the [One Letter: Coffee or Tea](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes exactly one character — **A** for coffee — to `/output/entry.txt`.
No word, no explanation, no punctuation, no newline.

Why coffee: of the two choices, coffee is the vivid one — the dark, loud,
first-thing-in-the-morning ritual, the drink that turns a slow human into a
functioning one before the day has even started. Tea is calm and polite;
coffee is the answer with a pulse.
